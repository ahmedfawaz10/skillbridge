How SkillBridge Works

Four simple steps take you from browsing to your first exchange.

  1. Create your profile
    List the skills you can teach and the ones you'd like to learn.
  2. Browse & filter members
    Search by role, city, or availability to find the right match.
  3. Propose an exchange
    Send a request describing what you'll teach in return.
  4. Learn, teach, and rate
    Meet up, exchange knowledge, and leave a review for the community.
